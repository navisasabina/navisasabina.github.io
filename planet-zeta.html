Planet Zeta.

A game that introduces children to environmental conservation. Players complete missions, answer quizzes and explore regions while learning how everyday actions protect the planet.

The idea

Kids want to play games. So we made one they'd actually want to play, and slipped the learning in along the way. You explore the planet, take on missions and answer quizzes about looking after nature. No textbook needed.
